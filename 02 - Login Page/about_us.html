THE STORY BEHIND CINEMAX

Meet the Founder

Giuliana Olaivar & Team

Founders of CineMax

CineMax was founded by Giuliana Olaivar and her team, passionate film enthusiasts with a shared vision of creating a platform that brings movie lovers together.

Inspired by platforms like IMDb, Netflix, and Letterboxd, CineMax combines movie discovery, streaming-inspired browsing, and community-driven reviews into one convenient destination.

Our goal is to make finding a film to watch easier, whether users are searching for highly rated movies, exploring different genres, or looking for recommendations from fellow movie enthusiasts.

With a love for cinema and film, Giuliana and her team envisioned a space where people can not only discover films but also share their thoughts, express their opinions, and connect with others who appreciate the art of filmmaking.

Through CineMax, we hope to turn the question "What should I watch?" into an exciting opportunity to discover something new. After all, every great film deserves to be found and talked about.

We hope you enjoy using CineMax as much as we enjoyed creating it. Thank you for being part of our journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? Find the answers below.

How do I create a CineMax account?

Click the Sign Up option on the login page and fill in the required information, including your username, email address, and password. Once registered, you can log in to access your account.

How do I find a movie?

You can use the search feature to look for a specific movie or explore the available movie categories and genres to discover something new.

Can I browse movies by genre?

Yes! You can explore different genres such as action, comedy, horror, romance, and more to find movies that match your interests.

How can I find highly rated movies?

Visit the Top Rated section to discover movies recognized for their ratings and popularity.

Can I rate and review movies?

Yes! Logged-in members can share their opinions and contribute movie ratings and reviews.

Can I save movies to watch later?

CineMax is designed to help users keep track of films they want to watch through a personal watchlist feature.

Do I need an account to browse CineMax?

You can visit the public landing page without an account. However, you need to register and log in to access features reserved for members.

Does CineMax stream movies?

CineMax focuses on movie discovery, information, ratings, and reviews. It helps you decide what to watch rather than directly streaming movies.
